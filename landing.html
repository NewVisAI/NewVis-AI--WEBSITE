Six modules, shown as they really are: screenshots of the NewVis dashboard and frames from the NewVis analytics pipeline. Nothing on this page is a mock-up.

NewVis · Live Cameras
ProductionReal dashboard

Every camera, on one screen.

Connect existing CCTV over standard RTSP and watch every feed live in one grid, filtered by floor, with the camera name and time on every tile.

  • Works with the IP cameras and NVRs you already have
  • Group and filter cameras by floor or location
  • Analyses a light sub-stream while the full-resolution stream keeps recording
  • Each role only sees the cameras and pages it is allowed to
NewVis · Live Video Analytics
ProductionReal output

Every person found, followed and counted.

NewVis detects each person in frame, gives them an anonymous ID and draws the path they take, so you see movement, not just snapshots.

  • Person detection and multi-person tracking in real time
  • Movement trails and live headcount per camera and per zone
  • The same person recognised across cameras as one ID Beta
  • Anonymous by design: IDs, not names, and no face recognition
NewVis · Notifications
ProductionReal dashboard

An alert with the evidence attached.

When a rule is broken, the right person gets a notification saying what happened, where and when, plus a snapshot of that exact frame with the person boxed. Switch between the inbox and a snapshot below the screen.

  • Restricted-zone, after-hours, loitering and occupancy alerts
  • Snapshot evidence saved with every alert
  • Jump to the recorded clip of the moment
  • Fall, running and altercation alerts Beta
NewVis · Zones & Rules
ProductionReal dashboard

Draw the rules once. NewVis enforces them 24/7.

Click out a restricted area or a counting line directly on the live camera view, then set when each rule applies: hours, school days and a holiday calendar.

  • Draw polygon zones and in/out counting lines on any camera
  • Per-zone active hours and school days
  • Holiday calendar, so a non-school day is treated as one
  • Every entry and exit logged for reports
NewVis · AI Search
BetaReal dashboard

Ask your footage a question.

Type a question in plain English. NewVis shows how it understood the question, then lists the matching events, each with a one-click clip.

  • Filter by camera, zone, person ID and type of event
  • Shows the parsed intent, so you can see what it searched for
  • Results link straight to the clip
  • Currently best at camera, zone and event questions; time-of-day questions are still being improved
NewVis · Reports & Heatmap

The cameras that watch for incidents also count. Headcount by hour, entries per zone and dwell heatmaps show which areas are busy, when, and where people linger.

  • Headcount by hour and entries per zone
  • Spatial heatmaps of where people spend time
  • Scheduled periodic reports and data export
  • Append-only audit log of who viewed or changed what

Why NewVis

Where NewVis is different.

Uses cameras you already own

Works with your existing CCTV over standard RTSP. No forklift upgrade, no vendor lock-in on hardware.

Affordable & edge-first

Runs on modest on-site boxes — even CPU or USB AI accelerators — with no per-camera cloud fees. Raw video never leaves the building.

True cross-camera identity

Follows one person across cameras and floors as a single ID — and respects a real camera map, so no impossible "teleport" matches.

Privacy-first by design

People are tracked as anonymised IDs — no faces, no names — behind role-based access, licensing and an append-only audit log.

Plain-English, not raw logs

Ask "show falls on camera 3" and read a written summary of activity — built for staff, not data engineers.

Cost-optimised, accuracy-preserving

A stack of efficiency levers cuts compute without trading away detection — we don't ship anything that regresses accuracy.
